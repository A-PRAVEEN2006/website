# FoodLens | Technical Documentation

FoodLens is a comprehensive health technology application designed for automated nutritional tracking and dietary analysis. Powered by the Google AGI food model, it provides users with instantaneous, high-accuracy data regarding their nutritional intake.

## Functional Overview

FoodLens streamlines the process of monitoring macros and micro-nutrients, replacing traditional manual logging with an AI-driven vision system capable of identifying food items and decrypting their metabolic data.

### Core Features

- **Instant Food Identification**: High-performance AI recognition of a wide variety of food items and complex meals.
- **Nutritional Decryption**: Accurate calculation of calories, proteins, carbohydrates, and essential fats.
- **Metabolic Tracking**: A persistent dashboard that monitors nutritional trends and overall health metrics.
- **Advanced Health Models**: Utilizes large-scale AGI models specifically optimized for nutritional science.

## Technical Specifications

- **Deployment Platform**: Android (APK)
- **Application Size**: ~96MB
- **AI Engine**: Google AGI Food Model
- **Connectivity**: Online Mode (Requires connection for nutritional database synchronization)

## Installation and Usage

FoodLens is available for download through the PRIME X platform. To use the application, simply point the device camera at the food item to receive a complete nutritional breakdown.
